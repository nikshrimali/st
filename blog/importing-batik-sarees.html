How to import batik sarees from India to Sri Lanka

Importing batik sarees from India to Sri Lanka can be a rewarding venture. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you through the process:

  1. Research and find reliable Indian batik saree suppliers in Jaipur.
  2. Contact the suppliers and inquire about their wholesale prices, minimum order quantities, and shipping policies.
  3. Request samples to assess the quality of the sarees.
  4. Negotiate the terms of the purchase and place your order.
  5. Arrange for shipping and ensure you have all the necessary import documents.
  6. Clear your shipment through Sri Lankan customs and pay any applicable duties and taxes.

By following these steps, you can successfully import beautiful batik sarees from India and offer them to your customers in Sri Lanka.

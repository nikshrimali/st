Jaipur batik vs. Sri Lankan batik – differences & benefits

While both Jaipur and Sri Lanka are known for their beautiful batik, there are some key differences to be aware of:

Jaipur Batik:

  • Characterized by intricate and detailed motifs, often inspired by nature and Rajasthani culture.
  • Uses a wax-resist dyeing technique that results in a unique crackled effect.
  • Often features vibrant and bold colors.

Sri Lankan Batik:

  • Typically features more geometric and abstract designs.
  • The wax is often applied with a tool called a "tjanting," which allows for more precise lines.
  • The color palette is often more subdued and earthy.

Benefits of Jaipur Batik for Sri Lankan Boutiques:

  • The vibrant colors and intricate designs of Jaipur batik can appeal to Sri Lankan customers looking for something unique and eye-catching.
  • By offering Jaipur batik, you can differentiate your boutique from others that may only carry Sri Lankan batik.
  • The quality and craftsmanship of Jaipur batik can be a major selling point for your customers.

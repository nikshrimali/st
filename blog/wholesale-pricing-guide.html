Wholesale pricing guide for batik sarees

Understanding wholesale pricing is essential for running a profitable batik saree boutique. Here are some factors that influence wholesale pricing:

  • Fabric: The type and quality of the fabric used will have a significant impact on the price.
  • Complexity of the design: More intricate and detailed designs will be more expensive.
  • Order quantity: Most suppliers offer discounts for larger orders.
  • Supplier: Prices can vary significantly from one supplier to another.

When contacting suppliers, be sure to inquire about their pricing structure and any available discounts. By doing your research, you can find a supplier that offers high-quality sarees at a price that works for your business.

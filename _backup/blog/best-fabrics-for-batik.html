Best fabrics for Sri Lankan batik boutiques

Choosing the right fabric is crucial for a successful batik boutique. Here are some of the best fabrics for Sri Lankan batik boutiques:

  • Mulmul Cotton: Soft, breathable, and lightweight, mulmul cotton is perfect for the Sri Lankan climate.
  • Cotton Cambric: A fine, lightweight cotton fabric that is easy to dye and holds batik patterns well.
  • Cotton Voile: A sheer, lightweight fabric that is perfect for creating elegant and airy batik sarees.
  • Silk Blends: For a more luxurious feel, consider silk blends that offer a beautiful drape and sheen.

By offering a variety of high-quality fabrics, you can cater to a wider range of customers and stand out from the competition.

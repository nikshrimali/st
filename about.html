The Heart of Handcraft

We are more than just a brand; we are custodians of a legacy.

Your Trusted Indian Batik Supplier

Shoolin Textiles was born from a desire to bring the timeless beauty of Indian textiles to the modern world. Our journey began in the small, vibrant workshops where artisans have practiced the ancient art of Batik for generations. As a leading Indian batik supplier, we partner directly with these craftsmen and women to create authentic, high-quality batik fabric.

Our passion lies in the intricate details—the delicate crackle of the wax, the rich depth of the natural dyes, and the soulful imperfections that make each piece unique. We are committed to fair trade practices and sustaining the communities that keep this art form alive.

The Magic of Mulmul Cotton

Alongside our batik, we have a deep love for the mulmul cotton saree. Known as 'woven wind' for its incredibly soft and light texture, mulmul is a fabric of unparalleled comfort and grace. We source the finest mulmul cotton to create sarees that feel as beautiful as they look. Whether it's a simple, elegant drape or a canvas for our mulmul cotton batik fabric, the quality of our materials is paramount.

At Shoolin Textiles, every thread tells a story. We invite you to be a part of it.
